# Every Language Runs on Verbs

**1. Language runs on verbs**

Most language-learning products start with vocabulary.

VerbBoard starts with a different assumption:

Every language runs on verbs.

Verbs carry action, time, intent, and structure. Everything else in the product follows from that assumption.

VerbBoard is a focused environment for learning how verbs actually work in a language -- their forms, their patterns, how they appear in real sentences.

Every verb in VerbBoard has a "board": a conjugation table organized by tense, person, and number, along with example sentences. Each form and example has audio.

The table makes patterns visible instead of implicit. Learners see not just isolated forms, but the system behind them.

The goal is not memorization. It is pattern recognition. Once a learner understands how one verb in a group behaves, the next one costs less.

Language learning is recognizing repeated systems, not collecting unrelated words.

A language is not a list of words. It is a system of transformations. Verbs are where those transformations are most visible, and therefore where learning should start.

---

**2. Some effort is exactly where learning happens**

One of the most common questions: why not translate every conjugated form?

It would be technically easy. I chose not to.

By the time a learner is looking at a verb in VerbBoard, they already have:
- the infinitive
- example sentences translated into their interface language on demand
- audio for every form
- the complete conjugation table

At that point, I leave some work for them.

See the examples. Recognize the patterns. Connect the dots.

Translation establishes meaning. Pattern recognition builds understanding -- and is what helps new material stick. These are not the same thing.

The same principle shapes guided practice. Advancing requires sustained engagement with each verb before moving on.

The barrier is intentional -- VerbBoard favors active exposure over passive browsing.

Many products optimize for reducing effort. Language learning is different: some effort is exactly where the learning happens.
